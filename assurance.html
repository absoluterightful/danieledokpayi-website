Behind the Song

The Meaning of Assurance

Assurance is about the kind of unshakable peace that remains when life gets noisy. We are all on a long journey, trying to stay strong, pursue our dreams, and handle whatever comes our way. I wrote this song as a reminder to myself, and to anyone listening, that when your faith is firmly rooted in God, you do not have to live in fear or constant worry.

I wanted the song to carry a meaningful message while still living over an Afrobeat groove you can drive to, dance to, and enjoy every day. At its heart, Assurance is about feeling protected, remaining steady, and knowing that God is with you no matter what life brings.

When your faith is rooted in God, you do not have to let the noise control your peace.
